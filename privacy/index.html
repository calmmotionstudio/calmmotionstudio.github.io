Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Application: Calm Motion Studio Organic Traffic Engine

1. About Calm Motion Studio

Calm Motion Studio operates an Etsy-based digital products storefront (https://calmmotionstudio.etsy.com) and uses Pinterest to publish and manage organic content related to its products.

2. Information We Access

The Pinterest integration may access information associated with our own Calm Motion Studio Pinterest Business account, including Pins, Boards, and performance analytics such as impressions, saves, clicks, and outbound clicks.

The application is intended solely to manage Calm Motion Studio's own Pinterest presence and is not designed to collect private information from other Pinterest users.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through the integration is used exclusively to:

  • Create and publish original Pins;
  • Organize Pins and Boards;
  • Monitor content performance;
  • Improve organic content strategy;
  • Understand which content generates engagement and traffic to our products.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information obtained through the Pinterest integration.

The application is not used to send automated messages, direct messages (DMs), comments, unsolicited communications, or fake engagement.

5. Data Storage and Security

Application credentials and business analytics are stored securely and used only for operating and improving Calm Motion Studio's content publishing system.

We do not store or process sensitive personal data of other Pinterest users, and no authentication credentials or tokens are published or exposed.

6. Third-Party Services

Calm Motion Studio uses third-party services including Pinterest and Etsy. The use of those services is also governed by their respective terms and privacy policies:

  • Pinterest Privacy Policy
  • Etsy Privacy Policy

7. Data Requests and Contact

For privacy questions, inquiries, or data-related requests, users may contact Calm Motion Studio directly through our storefront at:

https://calmmotionstudio.etsy.com

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as our services or integrations change. The latest version will remain available at this public URL.
